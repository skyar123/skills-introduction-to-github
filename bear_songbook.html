Teacher & Parent Guide

For Fragile Infants (NICU Graduates, Sensory Sensitivities)

  • Start with humming before adding words
  • Keep volume very low (whisper singing)
  • Watch for overstimulation signs: looking away, arching back, fussing
  • One sensory input at a time (just voice, no movement initially)
